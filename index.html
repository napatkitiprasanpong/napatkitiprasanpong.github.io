Closed-loop smart insole for diabetic foot ulcer prevention

An insole concept that senses pressure, temperature, and time, adjusts support with a pump and valves, then measures again to keep or reverse each change.

  • 11 pressure sensors, 6 temperature sensors, and 4 pneumatic bladder zones: the controller senses, confirms sustained load, plans, adjusts, verifies, then keeps or reverses each change.
  • A TypeScript simulator and controller with 17 automated tests and a web dashboard (scenarios, fault injection, CSV export) that plans offloading for 1 to 3 hotspots at once.
  • In simulation, heel pressure fell from 480 to 350 kPa (1 hotspot) and 480 to 354 kPa (3 hotspots). Every hotspot ended below the 400 kPa threshold in about 5 seconds, with no new hotspot.
  • A learning step refines plans from rejected adjustments, and hysteresis re-tests prevent cycling at the threshold.
  • Concept hardware: a 5-layer CAD model (270 x 85 mm, 6.6 mm stack) and an ESP32-S3 controller board with 16 pressure and 8 temperature channels, 9 MOSFET drivers, and a hardware E-stop.
  • A requirements and risk register, a comparison with Orpyx, Podimetrics, and Siren, and an 18-slide pitch deck on cost, risks, and business model.
  • TypeScript
  • Simulation
  • ESP32-S3
  • CAD
  • Medical device concept

Robotics team project: temi robot app for indoor air quality

I’m the programmer on a team with senior students building a Kotlin and Jetpack Compose Android app that turns a temi robot into a mobile indoor air quality inspector.

  • A Raspberry Pi sensor node sends 9 readings over MQTT (temperature, humidity, CO₂, PM2.5, PM10, TVOC, HCHO, light, sound) into a live IAQ score, dashboard, and trends. The robot drives configurable routes, samples each stop, and exports the results to CSV.
  • Computer vision: MoveNet pose estimation (17 keypoints, ONNX Runtime) detects hand-raise and T-pose gestures that call or stop the robot, with distance estimated from shoulder width.
  • A Sign Language tab recognizes 250 ASL signs (MediaPipe Holistic, 543 landmarks per frame, TensorFlow Lite), and the robot speaks each word at 60% confidence or higher.
  • Tested and debugged with teammates, using simulated-robot and simulated-sensor modes to test without hardware.

Personal project, open source

NeuroSight

A Rust simulation of vision-to-stimulation encoding. It turns webcam events, such as a wave of the hand, into simulated tactile and TMS-style phosphene patterns.

  • Perception stage: MoveNet SinglePose Lightning (ONNX) reads each webcam frame and returns 17 keypoints with confidence values.
  • Events such as waving are encoded into two simulated outputs: a tactile pattern and a TMS-style phosphene pattern.
  • Also the basis of the NeuroSight-FPGA proposal for CRAFT MAN CHIP 2026.

Lorekeeper Live

A screen-only demo for streamers: read the screen, save the moment, and build the channel’s lore. It captures a still frame from a browser tab or game window that you choose to share, then turns it into a searchable lore card.

  • Capture a frame through the browser’s native screen picker, then review it and add a title, recap, hero, map, timestamp, source link, and tags.
  • Search the lore archive, show a card on an OBS browser overlay, and preview a session recap.
  • Runs locally on Node.js with no dependencies, API keys, or Twitch login. Captures stay in the browser and nothing is uploaded.
  • JavaScript
  • Node.js
  • OBS overlay

Personal project, open source (MIT)

MatePet for temi

An offline AI companion for the temi robot. A VRM avatar on temi’s screen chats using a small on-device language model, and temi speaks every reply in its own voice. It also works with the IAQ monitoring app above.

  • Voice chat with no cloud: temi speech-to-text, a local SmolLM2 360M model running through llama.cpp, then temi text-to-speech.
  • Open apps by voice (for example “open YouTube”), and control the IAQ app: ask about the air, start or stop missions and routines, or switch screens.
  • Commands are matched by rules rather than by the small model, so they are fast and reliable. Targets temi V2 and V3.
